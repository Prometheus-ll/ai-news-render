Privacy Policy - AQ Poster

Last updated: November 17, 2026

AQ Poster is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the "AI Quantum" Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel to post the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What the app accesses

Only the Facebook Page and Instagram account owned by the app's developer, using the permissions needed to publish videos and read basic post information. The app posts videos that the owner's own automation has produced.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data about visitors, followers or any other people. The app keeps only a technical access token for the owner's own Page, stored as a secret in the owner's private automation settings.

Sharing

We do not share any personal data with third parties. Videos are published to the owner's own Facebook, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no user data, there is nothing to delete. To remove the app's access, the Page owner can remove "AQ Poster" under Facebook Settings > Business Integrations. Questions can be sent to the AI Quantum Facebook Page by message.
